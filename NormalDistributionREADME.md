### Normal Distribution In Numpy

<p><code>normal()</code> generates random number following: <br>
   <ul>
    <li>Values near the mean occur more frequently...</li> <br>
    <li>Extreme Values are rare...</li> <br>
   </ul>
</p> <br>

 <center><img src="https://images.ctfassets.net/kj4bmrik9d6o/5NXsrRGCJy3k48xEy9FzAx/66e3ac09165bf6710c3b08e001adffe1/Normal_Distribution_08.png" alt="Bell Curve Diagram" height="300px"></center>

 <br>
 <p>Syntax: <br>
  <code>
    np.random.normal(loc,scale,size)
  </code>  <br>
   loc --> mean  <br>
   Scale --> Standard Variation <br>
   Size --> Output Shape <br>
</p>
